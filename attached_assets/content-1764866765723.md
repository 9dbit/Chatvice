#### About NN 2026  Conference

Welcome to Connektiva, the premier conference for event professionals from around the world. At Connektiva, we believe that events are not just a gathering of people, but an experience that leaves a lasting impression.

That’s why we have brought together some of the brightest minds in the industry to share their insights and best practices for creating unforgettable events.

Over the course of our multi-day conference, you will have the opportunity to attend thought-provoking sessions, interactive workshops, and inspiring keynotes that will give you the tools and knowledge to take your events to the next level. Whether you’re an event planner, marketer, or vendor, there’s something for everyone at Connektiva.

### Meet the incredible speakers

Our speakers are experts in their respective fields, and their knowledge is infused with passion and expertise. They bring a wealth of experience, ready to share their stories, ideas, and transformative concepts with you.
