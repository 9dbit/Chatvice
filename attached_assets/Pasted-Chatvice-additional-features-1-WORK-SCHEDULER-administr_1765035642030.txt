Chatvice additional features

1. WORK SCHEDULER (administrator)
Schedule shifts with ease
Control when supervisor & ai agents accept chats with the work scheduler. Setting are shown at each page supervisor & agents
Easy shift planning (administrator)
The work scheduler allows you to set shifts for weekdays, weekends, or each day separately. You can also configure night shifts.
And shown the worker (supervisor & ai agent) report, work hour, shift, daily, weekly & monthly

2. ADMIN ROLES
Decide who’s in charge
Define roles and privileges for people on your team.
- Administrator
There’s only one owner. This person is in charge of your account and has access to all features.
- Supervisor
Supervisor focus on handling chats, so can only access pages : overview, agents, chat sessions & chat logs page. They have no access to the settings, knowledge base, widget setting, source, analytics and the reports.

3. Team activity (administrator)
Check when supervisor & agents are online and what status they have on. You will always know who is accepting chats and helping your customers.

4. Cards and carousels (feature on knowledge base page)
Cards that contain an image, a title, a description, and up to three buttons. Perfect for product recommendations. Add a few cards to create a carousel. Make a new feature (new tab on knowledge base page) name “product recomendation” fill product link url & automatically crawling image on that link & show picture as card cover image

5. Quick replies (for supervisor take over) (supervisor can access)
Pre-defined instant replies that can be selected by users. Convenient for scheduling, surveying, and suggesting possible answers. Usually use “/“ to
Choose saved reply message (give quick reply setting feature)

6. Buttons (supervisor)
A text message followed by buttons. Useful when you want to create a clickable response or build a menu. Can Setting what will offer to customer (button name & input url), ai agent can use this feature use trigger word validation & conversation needs

7. Never miss a chat
Sound and visual notifications from chatvice ensure that no chat or new message goes unnoticed. You can choose which notifications you want to receive.
Notifications setting on owner control panel setting:
Incoming chat sound, chat reply sound, angry customer sound. Can choose sound & upload new sounds

9. Welcome bubble
Saya mau membuat fitur pop up welcome message dengan 2 button seperti pada image attached, dalam halaman setting bisa di customize nama button dan url link nya. Automatically muncul ketika page reloaded, beri tombol x untuk close bubble
